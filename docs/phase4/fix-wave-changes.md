# What the fix wave changed (for updating `tests/scenarios`)

Behaviour that scenario steps marked `// BUG` (or `bug(...)`) may still assert in its old form:

- **Start units**: starting ground forces stand on the first home planet (the one with the dock), not in the system space; no false berth warnings at home. A rival's home has its space dock. A Milty string with 0 on a seat keeps the seated faction's home there (message "Home systems of the seated factions put on their empty seats: N"); a plain `96` is read as `96A`.
- **Off-map homes** (Creuss 51, Crimson 118) are drawn as an ordinary tile beside the map: `Tile 51` exists, starts a route through its gate, opens the big hex. A Creuss wormhole token is drawn on its tile.
- **Custodians token**: the first landing on Mecatol Rex shows a "Custodians token" payment block (6 influence, seeded automatically) in the landing block; one `custodians` warning only while less than 6 is paid; no `shortInfluence` "need 6, paid 0".
- **One cost**: the editor label, the shop counter and the automatic payment show the cost the check uses (Sarween Tools, AI Development Algorithm, Particle Synthesis, Hacan hero, laws). Naming a component in "Used" reprices an automatic payment. Winnu agent + Hegemonic Trade Policy add up. Keleres agent: the resource warning is "Allowed". Sol mech after Orbital Drop: a payment block for 3 resources.
- **Planet values**: summary totals, planet badges, tile labels and the shop's available counter include modifiers (Nano-Forge, attached cards, Helios, Titans hero); a changed number is gold. Mining Initiative and similar effects read the modified value.
- **Language**: the replay is the same in ru and en (notes return, relics purge). Russian: "Производство" in strategy editors, colour names in the prerequisite warning, "Второстепенная способность"; no raw `technology:iihq` / `components.choice.counts`.
- **Save**: technology specialties, component entry effects/payment, status spend survive reload and import.
- **Data**: technologies with one prerequisite have it (Gravity Drive, Dacxive Animators, Graviton Laser System, Bio-Stims, Self-Assembly Routines, Predictive Intelligence, Sling Relay). Researching another faction's technology warns (`researchForeign`). Winnu Reclamation and Lazax Gate Folding see Mecatol Rex with Thunder's Edge. All three Keleres own the shared components (agent starts unlocked). The starting technology choice is made on the players sheet (toggle buttons).
- **Hints and hooks**: hints of the steps "card", "transaction" and "round" are shown (strategy editor, transaction step, common part of a turn). A breakthrough can be taken in «Взято и сброшено» and its gain runs (Quantumcore gives Avernus; the expedition gives the breakthrough). Share Knowledge of a rival ends with the status phase. The status entry has a "Used" block. Mitosis was never double: the scenario landed 2 infantry.
- **Warnings**: a warning about a component named in "Used" stands inside the Used block of any editor (pass included), once. A deal with Hacan does not warn "neighbour" (Guild Ships). Saar: ground forces on any planet of the system with a Floating Factory do not warn. Arborec flagship produces up to 5 without false warnings. Fragment purge by Fabrication / Black Market Forgery does not warn. The fleet pool line counts Neuroglaive. A plan row reads "Component action: <name>".
- **Layout**: choice rows wrap inside the panel; a technology choice with more than 12 options is a slot that opens the component picker; plan tabs keep 190 px and scroll; nothing makes the panel scroll sideways.
- **Component inputs**: manual adjustment has a Commodities field; Doctor Sucaban offers per-planet "Remove one Infantry" buttons; "choose a player" of Umbat and Malleon offers me; the Nekro hero offers the technology; a relic fragment can be the component of a component action; the Arborec hero's editor has a Production block; Construction secondary of the Titans offers "A mech and an infantry instead of a PDS".
- **Numbers**: Lead From the Front spends the entered tokens; discarding Rally as a cost gives nothing; one Support for the Throne gives 1 point; Spy Net in a rival's entry offers the Yssaril hand; Dark Pact gives +1 trade good to each side; Hubris warns in a rival's entry; Political Favor removes a strategy token of its owner; Airo Shir Aur warns without another player's ships.

Left as it was (still true, keep the step): rival losses marked in my battle are written to the starting board; Hesh and Prit secondaries; Monument / Sway the Council cost; payment of another entry is not re-seeded when an earlier entry changes; shop tiles print the unit's printed price; `technology:asn` and `leader:argenthero` are hints.
